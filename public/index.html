THE RULES OF THE ROAD

Keep your options open.

↑ / W accelerates. ↓ / S / Space brakes, then reverses near a stop. ← → / A D steer. Esc / P pauses; R starts a new chase on a new road. Touch controls work too. You have full steering range. Turn-in is progressive, with quicker countersteering to catch slides; brake assistance reduces wheel lock-up.

Traffic keeps right. The other lane carries oncoming cars. Red lights stop ordinary traffic and release cars across the junction. The patrol follows you through traffic and onto the verge. On the vertical indicator, its blue dot approaches your orange dot as it gets closer.

Shoulders are bumpy and have less grip. Orange barriers, cones and hay bales can be pushed aside. Trees, roadside posts and traffic signs can be knocked down. Your suspension and chassis react to the road. Traffic impacts damage the car; overturning or being pinned at low speed lets the patrol catch you.

Open a 160 m gap for 5 seconds to escape. Survive longer and make clean close passes to build your score. Every new chase generates a fresh route. Best distance stays in this browser.

The player uses Suspension Lab vehicle physics. Other cars use a simpler guided model and yield during collisions. Hedges and buildings are decorative. This is a fictional driving game with representative handling.
