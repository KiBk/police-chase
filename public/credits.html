The cars

This work is based on Generic passenger car pack by Comrade1280, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Suspension Lab separated and normalised the ten vehicles, centred their wheel pivots, recessed the inner wheelhouses and added mechanical geometry, suspension simulation and elastic body deformation. Police Chase reuses those adaptations, adds patrol markings and lights, and gives the cars a procedurally generated world. The original car designs and textures are retained. No endorsement is implied.

The sound

Engine and tyre recordings by GiocoSound, TheLittleCrow, overmedium, mmplisskin, Tom Haigh (audible-edge) and qubodup. Full recording credits, licences and adaptations.

Suspension, landing, rolling, electric motor and the patrol's two-tone siren are synthesised. Sound is optional and starts only when enabled.

The engine

3D rendering uses Three.js (MIT). Player vehicle physics uses Rapier (Apache 2.0). The suspension and vehicle models were developed in Suspension Lab.

About the chase

All ten player cars have physical suspension, tyres and elastic chassis sections. Traffic and the patrol use simpler guided vehicle bodies and switch to dynamic collision response on contact. Movable barriers, cones and hay bales are physical objects. Trees, roadside posts and signs detach on impact. Hedges and buildings are decorative. Steering and brake assistance make keyboard control more forgiving. Car specifications, handling and chase rules are representative game choices, not measured production vehicles.

The game runs in your browser. Best distance is stored locally. No accounts, analytics or external asset streams.
